Rates are return on capital per day, capital = strike (cash-secured basis). The verdict fires when the redeploy rate beats the hold rate plus your safety margin. It does not check earnings dates, the replacement's delta (assignment odds), or liquidity; those stay your gates before you act. Enter the bid you'd actually get filled at, not the mid.
